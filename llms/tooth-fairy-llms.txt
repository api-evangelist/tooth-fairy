# Toothfairy

> Toothfairy (toothfairyapp.co.uk) is a UK dental telehealth platform — "the world's first dentist on-demand" — combining registered dentists with a mobile app for teeth straightening with clear aligners, teeth whitening prescriptions, and same-day emergency video dental consultations, available 24/7 and health-regulated in the UK. Backed by Seedcamp. Toothfairy is a consumer mobile product and publishes no public developer API.

## Product

- [Toothfairy](https://toothfairyapp.co.uk/): Dentist on-demand — straightening, whitening, emergency dental care
- [Teeth Straightening](https://toothfairyapp.co.uk/teeth-straightening/): Clear aligners with dental supervision
- [Teeth Whitening](https://toothfairyapp.co.uk/teeth-whitening/): Prescription teeth whitening
- [Emergency Dentist](https://toothfairyapp.co.uk/emergency-dentist/): Same-day video consultations
- [For Business](https://toothfairyapp.co.uk/for-business/): Employer dental plans
- [Our App](https://toothfairyapp.co.uk/our-app/): The Toothfairy mobile app
- [iOS App](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/tooth-fairy/id1542598398): Tooth Fairy on the App Store
- [Android App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.toothfairy): Tooth Fairy on Google Play
